## TL Studio Pro 1.3.0
- **Download Image & Video** – paste public links from YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram or X and save video (up to 1080p), audio (MP3/M4A) or a picture. Private and protected content is not supported.
- **AI Agent** – chat in plain words and let the agent make QR codes, convert documents, pictures and videos, fill PDF forms or download media. Bring your own Claude API key; it works only in a folder you choose and asks before every change.
- Khmer text for both new screens (draft; feedback welcome).

## TL Studio Pro 1.2.0 (formerly QR Studio Pro)
- **New name and home screen** – one app, a tile for each tool.
- **Convert Studio** – Excel/CSV to PDF, PDF to Word, PDF to Excel, Word to PDF, images to PDF and back, merge and split PDFs.
- **Convert Media** – convert, resize, crop and compress images; make and edit GIFs; video to GIF, convert, trim, extract audio.
- **PDF to Form** – find the blanks in a PDF, make it fillable, fill forms by hand or from a CSV.
- Khmer text for the new screens. Your settings and projects carry over; installing over 1.1.x upgrades in place.
