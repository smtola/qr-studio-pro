## What's new in 1.1.0
- **Faster, smoother UI** and a new premium look (Inter font, light/dark).
- **Live scan check** – a badge shows whether a test decoder can read your code.
- **New content types** – map location, calendar event, WhatsApp chat, Telegram link.
- **Batch generation** from a CSV file, and **print sheets** (A4 / Letter PDF with cut guides).
- **Design templates** gallery, **recent projects** with thumbnails, three new module shapes.
- **Khmer interface** (View > Language).
