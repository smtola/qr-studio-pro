## TL Studio Pro 1.2.0 (formerly QR Studio Pro)

- **Convert Studio** – Excel/CSV to PDF, PDF to Word, PDF to Excel, Word to PDF, images to PDF and back, merge and split PDFs.
- **Convert Media** – convert, resize, crop and compress images; make and edit GIFs; video to GIF, convert, trim, extract audio.
